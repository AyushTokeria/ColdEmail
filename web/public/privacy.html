ColdEmail privacy policy

ColdEmail is a personal tool used by a single person to send their own outreach emails. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks to send email from, and read email threads in, the owner's own Gmail account. It reads threads only to detect replies to emails it sent.

What it stores

Contacts, drafts, the resume and an encrypted Google refresh token are stored in the owner's own Cloudflare database. Nothing is shared with, sold to or used by any third party.
